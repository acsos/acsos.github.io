Venue Information

ACSOS 2025 will be held at the Tokyo International Forum.

March 10, 2025

Conference news and updates

We are delighted to announce that ACSOS 2025 will be held at the prestigious Tokyo International Forum in Tokyo, Japan!

About the Venue

The Tokyo International Forum is a multi-purpose exhibition center in Tokyo, Japan. Its unique architecture, designed by architect Rafael Viñoly, features a swaying glass hall that resembles a ship. It is located in the Marunouchi business district, right next to Tokyo Station.

  • Location: 3-5-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-0005, Japan
  • Accessibility: Directly connected to Tokyo Station (JR lines) and Yurakucho Station (Subway).

Accommodation

We have negotiated special rates with several hotels within walking distance of the venue.

  1. Tokyo Station Hotel (5-star) - The official conference hotel.
  2. Hotel Metropolitan Tokyo Marunouchi (4-star)
  3. Remm Hibiya (3-star)

Please visit the Venue & Travel page for booking links and discount codes.

Exploring Tokyo

Tokyo is a vibrant city that blends the ultramodern and the traditional. While you are here, take some time to explore:

  • Senso-ji Temple: Tokyo’s oldest temple.
  • Akihabara: The center of electronics and anime culture.
  • Imperial Palace: The primary residence of the Emperor of Japan.

We can’t wait to welcome you to this amazing city!
