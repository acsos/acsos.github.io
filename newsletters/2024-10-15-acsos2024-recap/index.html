ACSOS 2024 Recap

A look back at the highlights from ACSOS 2024 in Washington, D.C.

October 15, 2024

Conference news and updates

ACSOS 2024 has come to a close, and what a fantastic week it was! Held in the historic city of Washington, D.C., the conference brought together over 200 attendees from around the world.

Highlights

  • Keynotes: We heard inspiring talks on “Ethical AI” and “Biological Computing.”
  • Workshops: The workshops on Monday were well-attended and sparked lively debates.
  • Social Event: The dinner at the National Air and Space Museum was a highlight for many.

By the Numbers

  • 150 Submissions
  • 35 Accepted Papers (23% acceptance rate)
  • 5 Workshops
  • 2 Tutorials

Thank You!

We want to extend a huge thank you to:

  • Our Sponsors for their generous support.
  • The Organizing Committee for their tireless work.
  • The Volunteers who made everything run smoothly.
  • And most importantly, YOU, the attendees, for making this community so vibrant.

We hope you returned home inspired and energized. See you next year in Tokyo!
