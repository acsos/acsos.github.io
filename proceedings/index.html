ICAC and SASO Conference Proceedings (Pre-Merger)

Before the 2020 merger, ICAC and SASO published separate proceedings. The companion proceedings included workshops under various names: FAS*W (Feedback, Adaptation, and Self-managing Systems Workshops), SASOW (SASO Workshops), and ICCAC/CAC (Companion to Autonomic Computing).
